# Contributor terms

Short version: you keep your copyright, we can ship your work in this package, and it can never
become closed source.

There is nothing to sign. Opening a pull request means you agree to what is below.

## What you are agreeing to

**1. You give us permission to use your contribution.**
A perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, modify, distribute and sublicense what you
contribute, so it can ship as part of BaryoDev.Umbraco.Pwa and any project that includes it.

**2. That permission is limited to open source.**
Your contribution may only ever be distributed under a licence approved by the
[Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/licenses). This package is MIT today. If it ever
moves, it moves to another OSI-approved licence and no further.

We cannot take your work proprietary. Not "will not": the permission you are giving does not
extend that far, so there is nothing to change our minds about later.

**3. You keep your copyright.**
Nothing here transfers ownership. It is a licence, not an assignment. Your contribution stays
yours and you can use it anywhere else you like, including in commercial work of your own.

**4. Patents.**
If you hold a patent that your contribution necessarily infringes, you grant the same royalty-free
licence for it. This one matters here: MIT grants copyright permissions but says nothing about
patents, so without this clause a contribution could become a legal trap for everyone downstream.

**5. You have the right to contribute it.**
Either you wrote it, or you have permission from whoever did. If your employer owns your work,
please check before contributing. This clause exists to stop you accidentally causing yourself a
problem, not to catch you out.

**6. No warranty, and no obligation.**
Your contribution is provided as is. You are not signing up to maintain it, answer questions about
it, or fix it later. If you disappear tomorrow, that is fine and expected.

## What is deliberately not here

**No copyright assignment.** Some projects ask you to hand over ownership. This one does not.

**No right to relicense your work as proprietary.** The grant stops at OSI-approved licences, so
this package cannot be closed later. A permission we would never use is a permission worth not
holding.

**No signing step.** There used to be a bot asking contributors to sign an agreement that was not
in this repository at all, and its check was never required, so it blocked nothing while showing a
pending mark on every first pull request. Contributing is the agreement.

## Questions

Open an issue, or ask on the pull request. If something here worries you, say so before you spend
time on the work rather than after.
